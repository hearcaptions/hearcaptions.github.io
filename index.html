About HearCaptions

HearCaptions provides real-time captions for conversations without requiring internet access. It also includes optional audio amplification and clarity controls. The app is designed as an accessibility and communication support tool, helping users follow speech more easily in everyday listening situations.

Real-time captions Follow spoken conversations with live on-device captions.
Works offline Captions are generated without requiring an internet connection.
Audio controls Optional amplification and clarity controls for user comfort.

Important disclaimer

HearCaptions is not a medical device, hearing aid, diagnostic tool, or treatment app. It does not diagnose, treat, monitor, cure, or prevent hearing loss or any medical condition. Users with hearing concerns should consult a qualified healthcare professional.
